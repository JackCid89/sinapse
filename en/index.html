About SINAPSE

SINAPSE publishes on Tuesdays with the previous week's activity. Each issue has nine fixed sections: Pulse, Cover, Papers, Tech, Industry, Column, Counterpoint, Datum of the week, Appendix.

Editorial criteria — discipline rotation, geographic plurality of sources, rotating tones in the column — are detailed in the editorial guide. The visualization framework lives in viz-framework. The complete issue registry lives in data/registro.json.
