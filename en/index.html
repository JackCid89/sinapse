About SINAPSE

SINAPSE publishes on Tuesdays with the previous week's activity. Each issue has eight fixed sections: Pulse, Cover, Papers, Tech, Industry, Column, Datum of the week, and Technical appendix.

The editorial criteria — discipline rotation, geographic plurality of sources, rotating tones in the column — are detailed in the editorial guide. The complete issue registry, with disciplines and regions covered, lives in data/registro.json.
